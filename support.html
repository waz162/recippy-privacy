Recippy Support

Help with the Recippy iPhone app

Email Jack Li and you’ll get a reply about Recippy:

support.recippy@icloud.com

What to include

A sentence about what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead is enough. If the app misbehaved, the iPhone model and iOS version help too. You can find those in the iOS Settings app under General → About.

Recipes and your account

Recipes stay on your iPhone, and in your personal iCloud if you turn on sync. Recippy Plus and tips are billed by Apple. For a charge, refund, or subscription, use reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID that paid.

The privacy policy is at waz162.github.io/recippy-privacy.
